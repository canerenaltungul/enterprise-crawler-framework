# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone participating in the Enterprise Crawler Framework
community.

Contributors, maintainers, users, and other participants are expected to
interact professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Being respectful of different viewpoints, experiences, and backgrounds.
- Giving and receiving constructive technical feedback.
- Focusing discussions on ideas, code, documentation, and observable behavior.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and working to correct them.
- Respecting the time and boundaries of other community members.
- Helping maintain a collaborative and technically productive environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insults, threats, or deliberately hostile communication.
- Trolling or repeatedly disrupting technical discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate sexual content.
- Sustained off-topic behavior intended to derail discussions.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces associated with Enterprise
Crawler Framework, including:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Code reviews
- Project-related communication
- Other public spaces where someone is representing the project

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior related to the project,
you may report it privately to:

canerenaltungul.dev@gmail.com

Reports should include enough information to understand the situation,
including relevant links, messages, screenshots, or other context when
available.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues,
pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and frequency of the behavior, maintainers may take
actions including:

- Issuing a private or public warning.
- Temporarily limiting participation.
- Closing or locking discussions.
- Rejecting contributions.
- Permanently banning participation in project spaces.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on
protecting a safe and productive project environment.

## Good-Faith Technical Disagreement

Technical disagreement is not itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.

Participants are free to challenge architectural choices, implementation
details, documentation, project direction, or technical claims as long as the
discussion remains focused on the work rather than attacking individuals.
